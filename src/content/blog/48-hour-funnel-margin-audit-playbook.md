# The 48-Hour Funnel, Margin & Attribution Audit Playbook

Most emerging DTC brands and omnichannel retailers don't have a traffic problem — they have a **diagnostic problem**. Turning up paid social, creator seeding, or marketplace ads on top of thin unit economics or leaky product pages simply accelerates cash burn.

Before recommending a single dollar of working media spend, we run every storefront through a four-part diagnostic.

---

## 1. Contribution Margin & Break-Even ROAS Check

Vanity ROAS inside an ad platform means nothing if your landed unit economics can't absorb acquisition costs.

- **Landed Gross Margin:** After COGS, inbound freight, pick/pack/ship, and payment processing, brands generally need **55%+ gross margin** (minimum 50%) to scale paid acquisition sustainably.
- **Break-Even MER:** If your contribution margin before marketing is 50%, your absolute break-even Marketing Efficiency Ratio (MER) is `2.0x`. Targeting `2.8x–3.5x` gives real operating cushion.
- **AOV & Bundling Headroom:** Low-AOV single SKUs ($25–$35) often struggle on Meta/Google CPMs unless paired with bundles, tiered free shipping thresholds, or repeat-purchase replenishment.

---

## 2. PDP & Checkout Conversion Rate (CVR) Leaks

Sending $10,000/month of cold or creator traffic to a product detail page converting at `0.9%` wastes half your budget compared to a clean `2.2%` storefront.

We check five fast-win friction points:
1. **Above-the-fold clarity:** Does a cold visitor understand the core outcome, price, and differentiator in 3 seconds on mobile?
2. **Offer & Risk Reversal:** Clear shipping thresholds, transparent delivery windows, and authentic social proof near the Add-to-Cart button.
3. **Page Speed & Checkout Friction:** Bloated third-party scripts, forced account creation, or missing Shop Pay / Apple Pay accelerated checkout.

---

## 3. Signal Hygiene & Attribution Readiness

Post-iOS14 signal loss breaks automated bidding when ad platforms can't see real purchases:
- **GA4 + Server-Side CAPI:** Verify `Purchase`, `InitiateCheckout`, and `AddToCart` events fire cleanly without duplicate counting.
- **UTM & Creator Taxonomy:** Ensure every creator link, Whitelisting/Spark Ad, and affiliate code maps cleanly into one source of truth.
- **60–90 Day Ramp Expectations:** Account for pixel learning, creative iteration velocity, and affiliate activation lag across months 1–2 before judging steady-state MER.

---

## 4. Picking 1–2 High-Leverage Wedge Channels

Instead of spreading a `$1,500–$3,500/mo` retainer across six platforms at once, we isolate **1–2 wedge channels** matched to your ops:

- **High-margin visual DTC product:** Paid Social + UGC Creator Seeding / Whitelisting.
- **High-search-intent catalog or retail brand:** Google Shopping / PMax + Amazon Retail Media (Buy Box & TACoS discipline).
- **Collectibles, apparel, or surplus retail SKUs:** Whatnot / TikTok Live shoppable drops + eBay catalog syndication.
