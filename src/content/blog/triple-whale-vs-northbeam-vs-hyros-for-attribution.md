# Triple Whale vs Northbeam vs Hyros for Attribution

Attribution is the most important tool for scaling your Shopify ads – and the three leaders in this space are **Triple Whale**, **Northbeam**, and **Hyros**. Here's how they compare, when to use each, and real ROI data from stores using them.

## Quick Comparison

| Feature | Triple Whale | Northbeam | Hyros |
|---------|-------------|-----------|-------|
| **Primary focus** | Multi-touch attribution + ad analytics | Multi-touch attribution + optimization | Multi-touch attribution + ad tracking |
| **Shopify integration** | Native app, real-time sync | Native app, real-time sync | Native app, real-time sync |
| **Attribution model** | First-click, last-click, linear, time-decay | Position-based, time-decay, linear | Last-click, first-click, linear |
| **Ad platform integration** | Google, Meta, TikTok, Pinterest, Snap | Google, Meta, TikTok, Pinterest, Snap | Google, Meta, TikTok, Pinterest |
| **Dashboard** | Real-time, customizable widgets | Real-time, cohort analysis | Real-time, pixel-level tracking |
| **Pricing** | Free up to $50k/mo revenue | $99/mo (up to $50k) | $99/mo (up to $50k) |
| **Best for** | Stores wanting all-in-one analytics | Stores optimizing ad spend | Stores tracking pixel-level performance |

**Rule of thumb:** Use Triple Whale for all-in-one analytics, Northbeam for ad optimization, Hyros for pixel-level tracking.

---

## When to Use Triple Whale

Triple Whale is the **all-in-one analytics platform for Shopify stores**.

### Strengths
- **Real-time dashboard** – see revenue, ROAS, CAC, LTV in one place
- **Multi-touch attribution** – understand the full customer journey
- **Ad platform integration** – connect Google, Meta, TikTok, Pinterest, Snap
- **Cohort analysis** – understand customer lifetime value by acquisition channel
- **Forecasting** – predict revenue and ad spend based on historical data
- **Custom reports** – build custom dashboards for your team

### Weaknesses
- Attribution models are limited compared to Northbeam/Hyros
- Pricing can be expensive for larger stores (starts at $199/mo for $100k+ revenue)
- Learning curve for advanced features

### Best for
- Stores wanting a single dashboard for all analytics
- Stores needing cohort analysis and LTV tracking
- Stores with multiple ad channels (Google, Meta, TikTok, etc.)
- Stores wanting to forecast revenue and ad spend

### Setup Checklist
1. Install Triple Whale Shopify app
2. Connect all ad platforms (Google Ads, Meta Ads, TikTok, etc.)
3. Set up attribution model (recommend starting with "Linear")
4. Create custom dashboards for key metrics
5. Set up alerts for ROAS/CPA changes
6. Review weekly and adjust attribution model as needed

## When to Use Northbeam

Northbeam is the **ad optimization platform for Shopify stores**.

### Strengths
- **Multi-touch attribution** – understand which touchpoints drive conversions
- **Position-based attribution** – weight first and last touchpoints more heavily
- **Time-decay attribution** – recent touchpoints get more credit
- **Cohort analysis** – understand LTV by acquisition channel and cohort
- **Ad optimization** – recommendations for budget allocation and bid adjustments
- **Google/Meta integration** – sync data for automated optimizations

### Weaknesses
- No native email marketing (use Klaviyo for that)
- Pricing scales with revenue ($99/mo for $50k, $299/mo for $200k+)
- Less all-in-one than Triple Whale

### Best for
- Stores optimizing ad spend across multiple channels
- Stores wanting position-based or time-decay attribution
- Stores with complex customer journeys
- Stores needing budget allocation recommendations

### Setup Checklist
1. Install Northbeam Shopify app
2. Connect ad platforms (Google Ads, Meta Ads, etc.)
3. Set up attribution model (recommend "Position-Based")
4. Create custom reports for key metrics
5. Set up budget allocation recommendations
6. Review weekly and adjust based on performance

---

## When to Use Hyros

Hyros is the **pixel-level tracking platform for Shopify stores**.

### Strengths
- **Pixel-level tracking** – track every click, impression, and conversion
- **Last-click attribution** – understand exactly which ad drove the sale
- **First-click attribution** – understand which ad started the journey
- **Linear attribution** – give equal credit to all touchpoints
- **Real-time dashboard** – see pixel-level data in real-time
- **Ad platform integration** – Google, Meta, TikTok, Pinterest

### Weaknesses
- Limited attribution models compared to Northbeam
- No cohort analysis or LTV tracking (use Triple Whale for that)
- Pricing scales with revenue ($99/mo for $50k, $199/mo for $100k+)
- Less ad optimization recommendations than Northbeam

### Best for
- Stores wanting pixel-level tracking of ad performance
- Stores with simple customer journeys (one or two touchpoints)
- Stores needing last-click or first-click attribution
- Stores with smaller budgets (under $50k/mo revenue)

### Setup Checklist
1. Install Hyros Shopify app
2. Connect ad platforms (Google Ads, Meta Ads, TikTok, etc.)
3. Set up attribution model (recommend "Last-Click")
4. Create custom reports for key metrics
5. Set up alerts for pixel tracking issues
6. Review weekly and adjust attribution model as needed

## How They Work Together

The best setup for most Shopify stores:

| Layer | Tool | Purpose |
|-------|------|---------|
| **All-in-one analytics** | Triple Whale | Dashboard, cohort analysis, LTV tracking |
| **Ad optimization** | Northbeam | Budget allocation, bid adjustments, position-based attribution |
| **Pixel tracking** | Hyros | Pixel-level tracking, last-click/first-click attribution |

### Integration Example
1. Customer clicks ad → Hyros tracks pixel-level data
2. Customer visits site, adds to cart, purchases → Northbeam attributes touchpoints
3. Triple Whale aggregates data into dashboard
4. Ad platforms (Google, Meta) get optimized based on Northbeam recommendations
5. Store reviews weekly: adjust budgets, bids, attribution models based on data

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I need all three tools?**
A: No. Most stores under $100k/month revenue are fine with just one (Triple Whale or Northbeam). Larger stores may benefit from using all three.

**Q: Which tool is best for a small store?**
A: Triple Whale is usually the best starting point – it's all-in-one and easier to use.

**Q: How long does it take to set up?**
A: 2-4 hours for most stores, depending on the number of ad platforms and attribution model.

**Q: Can I switch attribution models later?**
A: Yes, but it will reset your historical data. Plan ahead before switching.
